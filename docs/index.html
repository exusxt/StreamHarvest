What it does

Everything a downloader should do — and more

Powered by yt-dlp, updated in-app, with a clean interface around it.

Everywhere support

Most major video and streaming platforms via yt-dlp — hundreds of sites, always updated in-app.

Format & quality presets

MP4, best-available, specific formats, and audio-only to MP3. Remux to other containers without re-encoding.

Clipboard monitoring

Copy a link anywhere on your system and StreamHarvest will ask if you want to fetch and download it.

Concurrent queue

Up to 8 parallel downloads with a reorderable queue. Batch imports from pasted lists or .txt files.

Playlists & channels

Load entire playlists or channels and tick exactly the videos you want. One failing video never blocks the rest.

Follow channels & playlists

Subscribe to a channel or playlist: new videos are checked in the background, then queued or notified automatically.

Download scheduling

Restrict downloads to a daily time window and switch to a slower speed limit during busy hours.

Subtitles & metadata

Download and embed subtitles, plus thumbnail and metadata embedding. Auto-merge and remux handled by ffmpeg.

Organized output

Files land flat or auto-sorted by site, date or playlist. Your folder, your rules.

14 themes

From sleek dark layouts to the photo-background Gallery Glass family — with a live preview grid.

Global hotkey

Summon the app from anywhere with Ctrl+Shift+D and instantly check your clipboard for a link.

Tray mode & notifications

Hide to the system tray so downloads keep running. Desktop notifications when a download finishes.

Advanced mode

Raw yt-dlp argument passthrough, download speed limit and proxy support for power users.

Self-updating engine

yt-dlp and ffmpeg are bundled and updated in-app — plus automatic app updates with one-click restart.

Three steps

How it works

From link to file — StreamHarvest handles the rest in the background.

Paste or copy a link

Paste a video URL into the app — or just copy one to your clipboard and StreamHarvest offers to fetch it.

Pick your quality

Choose a preset like "Best available", a specific format, or audio-only MP3. Pick individual videos from playlists.

Download

Queued downloads run concurrently with live progress, speed and ETA, then land organized in your download folder.
